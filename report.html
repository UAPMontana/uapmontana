This site does not collect reports. File yours directly with one or more of the organizations below; once it is public there, it can appear here.

NUFORC

National UFO Reporting Center, since 1974. Free online form; reports are reviewed and published publicly.

Mutual UFO Network. Reports get a case number and go to the state director and a field investigator. Full case files are member-only.

AARO (U.S. Department of Defense)

All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office. Its online form is for current or former U.S. government employees, service members and contractors with direct knowledge of UAP programs or events, not general public sightings.

Tips for a useful report

  • Write down date, time, direction, elevation angle and duration right away.
  • Keep original photo and video files; don't crop or filter them.
  • Check for aircraft (ADS-B Exchange, FlightRadar24), Starlink trains and satellites before filing.
  • Report your town, not your home address.

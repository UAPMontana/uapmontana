Live Sky Monitoring & Resources

Public tools for watching Montana skies, and for ruling out aircraft, satellites, meteors and aurora before you file a report. Every link was checked on 2026-10-27.

Aviation & weather cameras

  • FAA WeatherCams: Montana view — Live FAA aviation cameras at Montana airports and passes. Zoom the map to pick a site.
  • Montana 511 traveler information — MDT road and pass cameras statewide.
  • MDT Travel Info — MDT's road-condition and camera portal.
  • Glacier National Park webcams — NPS cameras, mostly daytime views.
  • Yellowstone webcams — NPS cameras, including the Montana gateways.

All-sky & meteor cameras

  • Global Meteor Network — Open network of meteor cameras. Check the station map for Montana and the region.
  • GMN weblog / daily data
  • AllSky7 Fireball Network — Network of all-sky cameras with seven lenses.
  • AMS fireball reports — American Meteor Society fireball report database. Use it to check whether a bright object was a meteor.

Sensor networks & passive radar

  • MADAR project — Multiple Anomaly Detection and Automatic Recording: a geomagnetic and EM sensor network.
  • MADAR III node map
  • MADAR live node status
  • RETINA passive radar network — Passive radar network.
  • Offworld Labs — The team behind RETINA.

Aircraft & satellite tracking (rule out the explainable)

  • ADS-B Exchange: Montana — Unfiltered aircraft tracking from ADS-B and MLAT.
  • airplanes.live: Montana
  • Flightradar24: Montana
  • tar1090 (open-source ADS-B map software) — Run your own receiver map.
  • Heavens-Above — Passes of the ISS, Starlink and other satellites, plus flares, for your location.
  • Find Starlink — Starlink 'trains' are a common source of UAP reports.
  • Satellite Map
  • NOAA SWPC aurora 30-minute forecast — Montana often gets aurora. Check here before reporting glowing lights to the north.
  • NOAA SWPC aurora dashboard

  • Skinwalker Ranch: Insider (live cameras) — Official live feed. Needs a paid Insider membership. Utah, not Montana.
  • Skinwalker Ranch: official site

  • NICAP — Historic case archive.
  • AARO (All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office) — U.S. DoD UAP office.
  • NUFORC
  • MUFON
  • Enigma Labs
